Convention for naming relations (Object Properties) 1. The Active-Passive Pattern (most relations) * Forward Relation: Active verb in the present tense (e.g., employs, teaches, owns). - Points from the independent entity (Whole), active agent (Actor), or creator to the dependent entity (Part), passive object (Target), or creation. * Reverse Relation: Passive voice or reciprocal verb (e.g., is_employed_by, is_taught_by, is_owned_by). Example: - Forward: Organization employs Person - Reverse: Person is_employed_by Organization 2. The Has-Is-Of Pattern (hiearchical relations) * Forward Relation: Starts with has_ to indicate ownership, composition, or property. * Reverse Relation: Starts with is_ or ends with _of to point back to the subject. Example: - Forward: Car has_part Engine - Reverse: Engine is_part_of Car Determines property direction based on priority: Forward relations point from the independent entity (Whole), active agent (Actor), or creator to the dependent entity (Part), passive object (Target), or creation.
